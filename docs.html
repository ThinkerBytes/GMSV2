The GreenMetrica Net-Zero Framework

A comprehensive guide to empirical emissions calculations, geospatial community mapping, and data-driven decarbonization roadmaps for educational and community institutions.

Three Pillars of Decarbonization

Our scientific methodology bridges personal accounting with institutional action.

1. Precision Carbon Accounting

Direct consumption of grid electricity, vehicle fuels, cooking gas, and dietary choices are quantified using empirical factors in standardized kg CO₂e units.

2. Geospatial Hotspot Identification

Regional emissions cluster along commute corridors. Visual pin plotting reveals neighborhood density zones to prioritize carpooling and bus transit routes.

3. Active Reduction & Circular Offsets

Residual footprints are actively balanced through solar generation credits and circular recycling offsets (-0.50 kg CO₂e/kg) to reach Net-Zero thresholds.

The Net-Zero Carbon Balance Equation

Mathematical formula governing total institutional and student carbon footprints.

Net Emission (kg CO₂e) = ∑ Gross Emissions − (∑ Recycled kg × 0.50)

Where Gross Emissions = Electricity + Car Fuel + Bike Fuel + LPG + Meat Diet + Dairy + Other Food + Waste Generation.

• Eco Champion < 350 kg CO₂e

Solar electricity users, low vehicle commute, active cycling or public transit, high waste recycling, and climate-conscious diet.

• Moderate Emitter 350 - 600 kg CO₂e

Typical household footprint with standard grid electricity dependency, moderate motor fuel usage, and average waste generation.

• High Emitter > 600 kg CO₂e

Elevated car fuel combustion, high grid electricity usage without solar mitigation, and zero or minimal material recycling.

Standardized CFPC Conversion Factors

Empirical coefficients derived from the Carbon FootPrint Calculator (CFPC) repository.

Material Recycling kg -0.50 kg CO₂e Avoided virgin resource extraction & circular offset credit

Excel Bulk Ingestion Format

How institutional administrators can prepare spreadsheets for instant bulk plotting.

The dashboard includes client-side Excel processing powered by SheetJS. To import data, use the downloadable template containing the following required columns:

SL No Name Class Latitude Longitude Electricity (kWh) Solar Car Fuel (Liters) Car Fuel Type Bike Fuel (Liters) LPG (kg) Meat (kg) Dairy (kg) Solid Waste (kg) Recycling (kg)

Note: If coordinates are omitted in user spreadsheets, GreenMetrica automatically applies a gentle regional cluster around the central coordinates (10.9069, 76.4094).
